The Long Version

Biography

Tarang Joseph is an accomplished singer, pianist, and songwriter from Bangalore, India. Known for his distinct voice and musically exciting yet accessible style, he fuses the richness of funk, jazz, and soul with the immediacy of pop — creating a sound that’s both nostalgic and refreshingly modern.

His musical journey started early. Tarang began learning classical piano at the age of four and trained for over sixteen years. By twelve he was already performing live, gaining years of experience across jazz, soul, singer-songwriter and pop — the apprenticeship that shaped his versatility as both a writer and performer.

Over the past five years, Tarang has released ten singles and an EP, earning placements on major editorial playlists and acclaim from Rolling Stone India, The Indian Music Diaries, A Humming Heart, and BBC’s Asia Network. A collaborative spirit, he’s previously teamed up with artists like Frizzell D’souza on Ecstasy and with RANJ & Mary Ann on Superhuman — named one of Apple Music India’s “100 Best Songs of 2022.”

His live career has taken off rapidly. Tarang has supported and shared stages with The F16s, Peter Cat Recording Co., Till Apes, Dot, Mali, and ZFEX (UK), performing at major events and festivals including Echoes of Earth. Backed by a stellar five-piece band — Adesh Vinod (guitars), Surya Kalyanaraman (bass), Shourjo Chatterjee (drums), and Gautam David (saxophone) — he has built a reputation for live performances that blend musicianship with energy and showmanship.

In November 2024, Tarang released his debut EP Liquid Sunshine, a nu-disco project that marked a new chapter in his sound. The EP was nominated for Best EP of the Year by The Indian Music Diaries and landed him the cover of Spotify India’s flagship English playlist Indienglish, cementing his presence in the Indian indie pop space.

Now, Tarang is gearing up to release his sophomore EP — an expansive, genre-blurring record that delves into funk, soul, nu-jazz, and alternative pop. The lead singles One Track Mind (Aug 2025) and Irresistible (Apr 2026) preview the project.

Blending the flair of icons like Michael Jackson and Jamiroquai with the modern sheen of Franc Moody and Parcels, Tarang Joseph is carving out a signature style — rooted in timeless music but speaking directly to now. With his sophomore EP on the horizon, he continues his mission to be a global voice and a flag-bearer for Indian indie alternative pop.
